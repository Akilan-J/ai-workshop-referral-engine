01Who we're targeting and what we promise

Who: 2027-batch B.E./B.Tech students in tier-2 and tier-3 colleges. All branches can join; CSE, IT and AI&DS come first.

Why now: November is placement season. Their most common gap is "I have no real project to talk about in interviews."

Promise: "Leave with a working AI project on your GitHub in 60 minutes, before your next interview." The pitch is the outcome, not "learn AI".

Timing: Sunday 11 AM, when there are no classes, labs or placement drives.

06Risks

  • Fake or self referrals: the system blocks self-referrals and duplicate emails or phones, and only shows masked names. The payout rule (final-year and attended) removes the incentive to cheat.
  • Group fatigue or admins removing posts: one post per group, made by a member of that group rather than an outsider. Second posts only go to groups that convert.
  • Registrations that never show up: calendar invite, two reminders, and prizes revealed live.
